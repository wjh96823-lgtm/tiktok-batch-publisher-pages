Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Information we process

When you authorize TikTok, the application may process your TikTok open ID, display name, authorization scopes, and OAuth access and refresh tokens.

How we use information

We use this information only to identify the connected account, maintain authorization, and perform publishing actions requested by you.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored on the application owner's system and are not sold or used for advertising. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect them.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is sent to TikTok only when required to provide the publishing service.

Your choices

You may disconnect your TikTok account and request deletion of stored account data by contacting the application owner.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the account or website where this page is published.
